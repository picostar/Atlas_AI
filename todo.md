# atlas_ai -- Roadmap

The kit's roadmap lives in GitHub Issues, not in this tree: https://github.com/picostar/Atlas_AI/issues

The `docs/agile/` files in this repository stay as placeholder scaffold on purpose. They are the templates an installed project copies, and `CLAUDE.md` treats placeholder agile files as the signal that a repository is the kit rather than a live project. Do not track kit work in them.

How kit work is recorded:
- One issue per item, with a `CU:` line in the body sized on the ATLAS Measure of Effort scale.
- Labels: `skill`, `pattern`, `process`, `docs`.
- Milestones group items by delivery goal. `Copilot rollout` holds what the first team rollout needs.
- The pull request that closes an issue carries the `Usage:`, `Model Fit:` and `Credits:` lines in its description, in place of a retro entry.

The ATLAS vision the roadmap serves: development process, skills layer, compliance governance, API-first architecture, domain-expert-led development, and operational sustainability. The gap analysis that produced the original items was migrated to issues #5 through #20 on 2026-10-28.
